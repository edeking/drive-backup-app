# Privacy policy

Drive Backup is a personal tool used only by its owner.

- It accesses Google Drive solely to upload, list and delete backup files
  that it created itself (Google Drive scope `drive.file`).
- It cannot see any other files in the Google account.
- It collects no personal data, and no data is shared with or sold to anyone.
- Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account under
  Security → Third-party access.
